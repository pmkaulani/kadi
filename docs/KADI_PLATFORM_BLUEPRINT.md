## Executive Summary
Kadi is a mobile-first digital invitation platform designed primarily for Kenya and global markets. It combines an immersive physical animated envelope reveal with a functional, editorial event website, guest household management, RSVP collection, and host tooling.

> **Visual source of truth:** Follow [`DESIGN_DIRECTION.md`](DESIGN_DIRECTION.md) for the current brand palette, logo direction and interaction design. Older palette/logo recommendations in retained source material are historical references and must not override it.

## Core Pillars
1. **Signature Animated Invitation:** A physical 3D envelope with opening flap, inner decorative liner, responsive wax/metallic seal, rising card emergence with layered shadows, and smooth transition to an interactive event website.
2. **Event-Specific Experiences:** Custom configurations for Weddings, Birthdays, Memorials/Funerals, Baby Showers, Graduations, Anniversaries, Corporate, and Community events. Dignified treatment for memorials (no celebratory confetti or playful motions).
3. **Household & Attendee Guest Model:** Supports individual and family invitations with controlled plus-one limits, meal/dietary preferences, custom questions, and high-entropy private tokens.
4. **Kenya-Centric Workflows:** Native WhatsApp sharing, phone formatting (`+254`), EAT timezone (`Africa/Nairobi`), KES currency formatting, and clear host payment instructions (M-Pesa Paybill/Till).
5. **Privacy & Security First:** Compliance with Kenya Data Protection Act 2019, ODPC guidelines, child data minimization, high-entropy token hashing, and append-only audit ledgers.
